A little about me

Building websites I actually enjoy using.

I'm a self-taught web developer. I learned HTML, CSS and JavaScript on my own, and I host my client sites on GitHub, which keeps hosting simple and low cost.

Before learning how to code, I worked in architectural drafting using AutoCAD. I soon realized that I could do more by combining my drafting skills with web development. I'm early in my career, and I treat every project as a chance to get better. I listen first, build what you actually need, and keep checking that the site feels comfortable and easy for your customers to use.
